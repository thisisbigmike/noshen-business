# Privacy Policy

**Effective:** <mark>[DATE]</mark>

<mark>[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]</mark> ("**NosHen**", "**we**", "**us**" or "**our**") runs NosHen, a service for buying and selling items in Nigeria where the buyer's money is held by a licensed partner until the buyer has the item. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you use NosHen (our website, web app, emails and support), why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 ("**NDPA**").

NosHen is the data controller for the personal data described here. Our Data Protection Officer is <mark>[DPO NAME]</mark>, at <mark>[PRIVACY EMAIL]</mark>.

## 1. The personal data we collect

### From you

- **Account data:** your email address, your full name, and the settings you choose. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google, and nothing else from your Google account.
- **Identity check data (sellers, and buyers on larger deals):** the NIN or BVN you enter, which we send to our identity provider to check. We keep the result, the verified name and the provider's reference, not the full number.
- **Bank account data:** the bank and account number you add for payouts or refunds, and the account name returned by the bank check. We store account numbers encrypted.
- **Listing data:** photos, descriptions, category, price, condition and area, and, where the category asks for one, the item's IMEI, serial number or chassis number.
- **Deal data:** your buy requests, deals, deadlines, release and refund history, and ratings you give and receive.
- **Messages and evidence:** chat messages in your deals, and photos and notes you add when a problem is reported.
- **Support data:** what you tell us when you contact us.

### From others

- **Our payment partner:** confirmation of payments, including the name, bank and account number of the account a payment came from, and the status of payouts and refunds.
- **Our identity provider:** the result of your NIN or BVN check.
- **Google, if you sign in with it:** your name and email address.
- **Other users:** what the other person in your deal says or uploads about the deal, and ratings they give you.
- **Links you arrive from:** if you sign up through a listing link or a friend's invitation, which link or person brought you.

### Automatically

- **Device and usage data:** your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, and times of access, kept in our logs for security and to run the Service.
- **Analytics data, only if you agree:** which screens you use and how you move through them, recorded with PostHog, including short recordings of your screen activity. What you type in fields for bank accounts, release codes, sign-in codes, ID numbers and payments is never recorded.
- **Cookies:** see our [Cookie Policy](/cookies).

We do not ask for sensitive personal data as defined by the NDPA (such as health, religion or political views), and we ask that you don't put it in listings or messages.

## 2. Why we use it, and our lawful basis

The NDPA (section 25) requires a lawful basis for each use of personal data. Ours are:

| What we do | Data used | Lawful basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create and run your account, and sign you in | Account data, device data | Performance of our contract with you |
| Run protected deals: payments, release codes, payouts, refunds, notifications | Account, bank account, deal, payment partner data | Performance of our contract with you |
| Check sellers' identity and bank account names | Identity check and bank account data | Performance of our contract; compliance with our partner's and our legal obligations <mark>[confirm]</mark>; your consent to the identity check |
| Decide disputes | Deal data, messages, evidence, listing data | Performance of our contract with both users |
| Prevent fraud, stolen goods and money laundering; keep the Service secure | All of the above, device data | Compliance with legal obligations; our legitimate interest in keeping users and their money safe |
| Answer questions and complaints | Support data, deal data | Performance of our contract; legitimate interests |
| Improve NosHen and learn which channels bring users | Usage data, how you arrived, deal data (in summary form where possible) | Our legitimate interest in improving the Service |
| Analytics and screen recordings with PostHog | Analytics data, device data | Your consent, which you can withdraw at any time |
| Send you news and offers | Email address | Your consent, which you can withdraw at any time |
| Meet legal, tax and accounting duties, and respond to lawful requests | Deal, payment and identity check data | Compliance with legal obligations |

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have checked that they are not outweighed by your rights. You can ask us about this.

## 3. Automated decisions

Some fraud checks run automatically, for example when a payment comes from an account in a different name. These checks only hold a deal for a person to review; a NosHen reviewer makes the decision. Under the [Protected Deal Terms](/deal-terms), a deal is released automatically if a buyer neither confirms nor reports a problem within {{delivery_hours}} hours of delivery; this follows the deal rules both users agreed to.

## 4. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with:

- **The other person in your deal:** your first name, whether you are verified, your ratings and number of completed deals, your listing, your messages in that deal, and, if a problem is reported, the evidence you add. We never show your email address to the other person, and a buyer's release code is never shared with anyone by us.
- **Our payment partner,** <mark>[PAYMENT PARTNER]</mark>, to collect, hold, release and refund money, and to meet its own legal duties, including anti-money-laundering checks.
- **Our identity provider,** <mark>[ID PROVIDER]</mark>, to check NIN or BVN.
- **Service providers** that work for us under contract: hosting, file storage and security (Cloudflare), our database (Neon), product analytics and error tracking (PostHog, in its EU region), sign-in (Google, only if you use it), email delivery (<mark>[EMAIL PROVIDER]</mark>) and support tools. They may only use the data to provide their service to us.
- **Professional advisers,** such as lawyers, auditors and insurers.
- **Authorities:** the police, courts, regulators (including the Nigeria Data Protection Commission and financial regulators) and others, where the law requires it or where we believe in good faith it is needed to prevent fraud or protect someone's safety.
- **A business that takes over NosHen,** if NosHen is sold, merged or reorganised, under the same protections as this Privacy Policy.

## 7. How we protect it

We use encryption in transit and at rest; bank account numbers are encrypted separately; release codes and sign-in codes are stored only in a form that can't be read back; staff access is limited by role, protected by two-factor sign-in and logged. No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will report it to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, as the NDPA (section 40) requires, and tell you if the risk to you is high.

## 8. Your rights

Under the NDPA, you can ask us to:

- **give you a copy** of your personal data and information about how we use it;
- **correct** data that is wrong or incomplete;
- **delete** your data, where we don't need to keep it for a deal, a dispute or the law;
- **restrict** how we use your data while a concern is checked;
- **object** to uses based on our legitimate interests, and to marketing at any time;
- **move** data you gave us, in a common electronic format;
- **withdraw consent** where we rely on it, without affecting what we did before; and
- **not be subject to a decision based only on automated processing** that significantly affects you.

To use these rights, contact <mark>[PRIVACY EMAIL]</mark>. We may ask you to confirm your identity, usually with a code sent to your email. We will reply within <mark>[30 days]</mark>. If we can't do what you ask, we will tell you why.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission** (ndpc.gov.ng). We would appreciate the chance to resolve it first.

## 9. Children

NosHen is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data about children. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.
