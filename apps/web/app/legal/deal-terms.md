# Protected Deal Terms

**Last updated:** <mark>[DATE]</mark>

These Protected Deal Terms ("**Deal Terms**") explain how a protected deal works on NosHen: how the buyer's money is held, when it is released to the seller, and what happens when something goes wrong. They form part of the NosHen [Terms of Use](/terms). If the two conflict on how a deal works, these Deal Terms apply.

In these Deal Terms, "**NosHen**", "**we**" and "**us**" mean the NosHen team (company registration in progress); the "**buyer**" and "**seller**" are the two users in a deal; and the "**Partner**" is <mark>[PAYMENT PARTNER]</mark>, the licensed financial institution that holds deal money.

## 1. What a protected deal is

1.1. A protected deal is a sale of one item between a buyer and a seller who use NosHen. The buyer pays into an account held by the Partner. The money stays there until the buyer releases it to the seller, the delivery window passes, or NosHen decides a dispute, as set out below.

1.2. The sale is between the buyer and the seller. NosHen is not the seller, does not own, inspect or guarantee any item, and is not an agent of either side for the sale itself. NosHen's role is to run the deal steps in these Deal Terms and to instruct the Partner where the money goes.

1.3. Protected deals are available for items listed on NosHen in the categories shown in the app, between users in Nigeria, paid in naira by bank transfer. Some items can't be sold at all: see the [prohibited items](/prohibited-items).

## 2. Who holds the money

2.1. Money paid into a deal is held by the Partner, not by NosHen. NosHen is not a bank and does not take deposits. <mark>[Lawyer to confirm the description once the Partner and holding arrangement are agreed.]</mark>

2.2. Held money does not earn interest for either user.

2.3. NosHen keeps its own record of every naira held, released and refunded for each deal, and checks it against the Partner's records every day.

## 3. Starting a deal

3.1. **Request.** A buyer starts a deal by sending a buy request from a listing, choosing a meet-up or delivery. The request shows the price, the NosHen fee and the total.

3.2. **Acceptance.** The seller has {{request_hours}} hours to accept or decline. A request that is not answered in time expires. The buyer can cancel a request before the seller answers. Accepting one request declines any others on the same listing.

3.3. **Terms are fixed.** Once the seller accepts, the price, fee, who pays the fee and the hand-over method can't be changed. To change them, cancel the deal and start a new one.

## 4. Paying

4.1. After acceptance, NosHen shows the buyer a bank account for the deal and the exact amount to pay. The buyer must pay that exact amount, by bank transfer, from a bank account in the buyer's own name, within {{payment_hours}} hours. A deal that isn't paid in time is cancelled.

4.2. A payment counts only when the Partner confirms it to NosHen. A screenshot, an alert or a message from the buyer does not count. Sellers should never hand over an item until NosHen shows the money as held.

4.3. If the amount paid is different from the amount due, or the paying account's name does not match the buyer's, the deal is held for review. A NosHen reviewer will contact the buyer, and may refund the payment instead of continuing the deal.

## 5. The fee

5.1. NosHen charges a fee of {{fee_percent}}% of the price, at least {{fee_min}} and at most {{fee_max}}, shown before anyone pays. The buyer pays it unless the seller chose to cover it, in which case it is taken from the seller's payout.

5.2. If a deal is cancelled before hand-over, the buyer gets everything back, fee included. If a dispute is decided in the buyer's favour, the buyer is refunded in full, fee included. <mark>[Proposed; confirm.]</mark> Once money is released to the seller, the fee is not refundable.

5.3. NosHen may change the fee for future deals by giving notice in the app. The fee for a deal is the one shown when it was accepted.

## 6. Hand-over

6.1. The seller must hand over the item within {{handover_hours}} hours of the payment being confirmed. If the hand-over hasn't happened by then, the buyer may cancel and be refunded in full.

6.2. **At a meet-up.** The buyer checks the item first, using the checks the app shows for that kind of item. If satisfied, the buyer gives the seller the release code shown in the buyer's NosHen app, and the seller enters it.

6.3. **By delivery.** The seller sends the item and marks the deal Delivered in the app. The buyer then has {{delivery_hours}} hours to tap Received or Report a problem.

6.4. The seller is responsible for the item until the buyer receives it, including delivery costs and risk unless the buyer and seller agree otherwise in the deal chat.

## 7. The release code

7.1. Each paid deal has a 6-digit release code, shown only in the buyer's app and never sent by SMS or email.

7.2. **Giving the code releases the money.** When the seller enters the correct code, the money is released to the seller. The buyer should only give the code after checking the item and being satisfied with it. Giving the code is the buyer's confirmation that the item was received as described.

7.3. NosHen will never ask for a release code. Do not share it with anyone except the seller, at the hand-over.

7.4. After {{code_attempts}} wrong codes, entry is locked for {{code_lock_minutes}} minutes and a NosHen reviewer is told.

## 8. Release

8.1. Money is released to the seller when one of these happens first:

- (a) the seller enters the correct release code;
- (b) the buyer taps Received after a delivery;
- (c) {{delivery_hours}} hours pass after the seller marked the deal Delivered, and the buyer has not reported a problem; or
- (d) a NosHen reviewer decides a dispute in the seller's favour.

8.2. **Release is final.** Once money is released, the deal cannot be disputed or reversed through NosHen. Any claim the buyer has against the seller after release is between them, and nothing in these Deal Terms removes rights the buyer has under Nigerian law. <mark>[Lawyer to confirm this clause is enforceable against consumers under the FCCPA 2018.]</mark>

8.3. After release, NosHen instructs the Partner to pay the seller's share to the seller's verified bank account. We aim to start the payout within 5 minutes, but the timing depends on the Partner and the banks.

## 9. Cancelling

9.1. Before hand-over, either the buyer or the seller can cancel a deal in the app. If the buyer has paid, the buyer is refunded in full.

9.2. A seller who often cancels paid deals may have their account limited or closed.

## 10. When something is wrong

10.1. **Reporting.** If the item is not as described, does not work, is fake, is locked to someone else's account, has a different IMEI, serial or chassis number from the listing, comes with papers that don't match, or doesn't arrive, the buyer can report a problem in the app at any time before the money is released. The money is frozen at once.

10.2. **Evidence.** Both sides have {{evidence_hours}} hours from the report to add photos, notes and anything else that shows what happened. The deal's chat, timeline and listing are also used.

10.3. **Decision.** A NosHen reviewer decides within {{decision_hours}} hours of the report, either to refund the buyer or to release the money to the seller, and tells both people the reason. Where a refund depends on the item being returned, the reviewer will say how and by when. <mark>[Decide the return process.]</mark>

10.4. **What the decision covers.** The reviewer decides only where the held money goes, based on the evidence and these Deal Terms. It is not a court judgment. <mark>[Lawyer to advise on whether and how users may challenge a decision.]</mark>

10.5. A buyer who makes false reports, or a seller who misdescribes items, may have their account limited or closed.

## 11. Refunds

11.1. Refunds are paid to the bank account the payment came from, or to another account in the buyer's name that the buyer gives us. We aim to start a refund within <mark>[1 business day]</mark> of the decision or cancellation, but the timing depends on the Partner and the banks.

## 12. Checks and limits

12.1. Sellers must verify their identity (NIN or BVN) and a bank account in their own name before they can be paid.

12.2. To keep deals safe, NosHen sets limits. At present: deals between {{deal_min}} and {{deal_max}}; a new seller's first {{new_seller_deals}} deals up to {{new_seller_max}} each; buyers must verify their identity for deals over {{buyer_id_above}}. NosHen may change these limits, or set different limits for an account.

12.3. NosHen may hold a deal for review, refuse a deal, freeze an account, or report activity to the Partner or the authorities, where we reasonably suspect fraud, stolen goods, money laundering or a breach of these Deal Terms.

## 13. Items that are not allowed

Items on the [prohibited items](/prohibited-items) list can't be sold through NosHen, including anything stolen, fake, or locked to someone else's account. A seller who lists stolen goods may be reported to the police.

## 14. Paying outside NosHen

If a buyer pays a seller in any way other than the deal's account, that payment is not protected and NosHen can't help recover it. The app warns both people when a chat message asks for payment outside NosHen.

## 15. Meeting safely

Meet in a public place, in daylight, and check the item before giving the release code. NosHen is not present at meet-ups and cannot control what happens there. Report any threat or crime to the police. Our [safety tips](/safety) say more.

## 16. What NosHen is responsible for

NosHen will follow these Deal Terms, keep an accurate record of every deal's money, tell both people about every change in their deal, and decide disputes fairly and on time. NosHen's liability is set out in the [Terms of Use](/terms), except that nothing limits our duty to account for and return money held for a deal.

## 17. Changes to these Deal Terms

We may update these Deal Terms. We will tell users by email or in the app before changes take effect. A deal already accepted continues under the Deal Terms in force when it was accepted.
