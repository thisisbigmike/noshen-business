# Prohibited items

**Last updated:** <mark>[DATE]</mark>

You can sell almost anything you can hand over in Nigeria on NosHen. These things can't be listed or sold through NosHen. If you see one, tell us at <mark>[SUPPORT EMAIL]</mark>. Selling them breaks our [Terms of Use](/terms), and we may close the account and tell the police. <mark>[Lawyer to confirm the list against Nigerian law.]</mark>

## Never allowed

- **Stolen goods,** or anything you don't own or have the right to sell.
- **Fakes and counterfeits:** copies of branded goods sold as original, including hair sold as human hair when it isn't.
- **Phones, laptops and other devices that are blocked, blacklisted, or locked** to someone else's Apple ID, Google account or other account.
- **Weapons:** guns, ammunition, parts for them, explosives, and knives or other items made to hurt people.
- **Drugs:** illegal drugs and anything for using them, and prescription medicines.
- **Live animals,** and products made from protected wildlife.
- **Dangerous goods:** chemicals, fireworks, gas cylinders and anything else unsafe to carry or store at home.
- **Cars and vehicles without genuine papers,** or whose chassis number doesn't match the papers.
- **Documents and identities:** passports, ID cards, licences, registered SIM cards, bank accounts, and anyone else's personal data.
- **Money and money-like things:** cash, foreign currency, gift cards, airtime and data bundles, cryptocurrency and investment schemes.
- **Adult content and services.**
- **Anything else that is illegal to sell in Nigeria.**

## Not supported yet

NosHen protects the sale of an item that is handed over. These don't fit a protected deal, so they can't be listed for now:

- **Property:** land, houses, rent and deposits.
- **Services,** jobs and events.
- **Food and drinks,** and anything with a short shelf life.

## Why these rules exist

Every deal on NosHen holds the buyer's money until they have the item. These rules keep buyers and sellers safe, keep NosHen within the law, and keep our partner bank willing to hold the money.
