# Cookie Policy

**Last updated:** <mark>[DATE]</mark>

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. NosHen uses as few as it can. This page lists them. It is part of our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## 1. Cookies that run NosHen and remember your choices

These keep you signed in, keep your account safe and remember the theme you chose. You can block them in your browser settings, but then you won't be able to sign in.

| Cookie | What it does | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `better-auth.session_token` (called `__Secure-better-auth.session_token` on the live site) | Keeps you signed in. It can't be read by scripts on the page. | 30 days, or until you sign out |
| `better-auth.two_factor` | Staff only: holds the gap between the password and the authenticator code at staff sign-in | A few minutes |
| `better-auth.state` | Only while you sign in with Google: protects the hand-off between Google and NosHen | A few minutes |
| `noshen-theme` | Remembers whether you chose the light or dark theme. Set only when you use the theme switch. | 1 year |

## 2. Analytics cookies, only if you agree

NosHen doesn't use analytics cookies today. When we add product analytics (PostHog, which stores data in the European Union), we will ask you first, and you will be able to change your choice at any time in Account. Nothing will be recorded from fields for bank accounts, release codes, sign-in codes, ID numbers or payments. <mark>[Lawyer to confirm the consent wording and banner under the NDPA and the GAID 2025.]</mark>

## 3. No advertising cookies

We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we don't sell your data.
